Reporting a Problem

If a portrait failed to generate, returned an unexpected result, or the app behaved incorrectly, please include the following in your email so we can resolve it quickly:

  • Your device model and operating system version;
  • The version of RetroMe, shown on the Settings screen;
  • The look you selected and approximately when you tried it;
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.

Please do not attach the photograph you used unless we specifically request it. We can usually diagnose the issue without it, and we prefer not to hold a copy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my photos stored on your servers?

No. Your photograph is processed and immediately discarded, and the generated portrait is returned directly to your device without being saved. The only copy of any image is the one on your phone. Full detail is in our Privacy Policy.

Where are my saved portraits kept?

Portraits you keep are stored locally on your device within the app. There is currently no cloud backup. If you uninstall the app or switch devices, they will not be retained unless you have separately saved them to your photo library.

Can I use the app offline?

No. Generating a portrait requires an internet connection, because the image is produced by a processing service rather than on your device. Portraits you have already saved can be viewed offline.

The portrait does not look like me. Why?

Results depend heavily on the photograph you provide. A clear, well-lit, front-facing photo where your face is prominent produces the closest likeness. Group photos, distant shots, heavy shadows, sunglasses, and profile angles all reduce accuracy.

My photo was rejected. What does that mean?

The image processing service declines photographs that are unclear, too small, or that fall outside its content policy. Trying a different photograph usually resolves it. A rejected request does not generate an image.

How do I delete everything the app has stored?

Open the Settings screen and choose the option to erase your data. This removes all saved portraits and returns the app to its initial state. Uninstalling the app has the same effect. This action cannot be undone.

Can I use the portraits commercially?

Yes, subject to our Terms and Conditions and to the rights of anyone appearing in the image. You must not present a generated portrait as an authentic photograph of a real event.
