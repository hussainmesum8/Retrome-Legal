Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 12, 2026  ·  Last Updated: September 26, 2026

RetroMe respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we process when you use the RetroMe mobile application (the "App"), why we process it, and the choices available to you. We collect only what is necessary to provide the App, we do not require an account, and we never sell your personal information.

1. Information We Process

Photos you provide. When you create a portrait, the photo you select, together with any style options you choose, is processed to generate your portrait.

Purchase and usage information. An anonymous identifier assigned by our subscription management provider, used to confirm your subscription status and apply the portrait allowance of your plan.

Technical information. Your IP address, used briefly to protect the service against abuse; aggregated, anonymous usage statistics; and crash and diagnostic reports that help us keep the App reliable.

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, or precise location, and the App does not require you to create an account.

2. How We Use Information

  • To generate the portraits you request;
  • To provide subscriptions and apply plan allowances;
  • To maintain the security and availability of the service;
  • To understand how the App is used and to diagnose and fix problems.

3. Photo Processing and Retention

Your photo is transmitted over an encrypted connection to our server and to a third-party artificial intelligence service provider, solely to generate your portrait. Photos are processed in memory and are not stored on our servers. The finished portrait is held temporarily, for no longer than ten minutes, so that the App can retrieve it, and is then deleted. Portraits you choose to keep are stored only on your device.

4. Service Providers

We rely on trusted service providers that process information on our behalf and only for the purposes described in this policy: artificial intelligence image generation, subscription management, analytics and crash reporting, and secure server hosting. Payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play; we never receive your payment card details. These providers handle information in accordance with their own privacy policies and applicable law.

5. Data Retention

Photos are deleted as soon as your portrait is generated. Records of your portrait allowance, linked only to an anonymous identifier, are deleted automatically after seven days, and subscription status records after thirty days. We do not maintain user profiles.

6. Advertising and Tracking

The App contains no advertising. We do not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies, and we do not sell or share your personal information for advertising purposes.

7. Your Choices

You can delete individual portraits in the App, erase all App data from Settings, or uninstall the App at any time. Because we do not operate accounts or store your photos, we hold no personal profile to access, correct, or export. If you have a request regarding your information, please contact us.

8. Security

All communication between the App and our servers is encrypted in transit, and access to our systems is restricted. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at hussainmesum8@gmail.com.
