Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 12, 2026  ·  Last Updated: September 12, 2026

RetroMe is built with your privacy as a priority. We do not sell your personal data, we do not run third-party advertising, and we do not share your information with outside parties except as described in this policy. Your photographs are never stored on our servers, and the portraits you create are kept only on your device.

1. Who We Are

RetroMe ("we," "our," or "us") operates the RetroMe mobile application available on iOS and Android. If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you may contact us at hussainmesum8@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the minimum information necessary to operate the App effectively.

Information you provide directly:

  • The photograph you select for a portrait, which is processed and then discarded, as described in Section 4;
  • Any optional text description you enter to guide the style of a portrait;
  • Any names you optionally enter when creating a group portrait;
  • Any messages or inquiries you send to us via email.

Information stored locally on your device:

  • The portraits you choose to keep, stored as image files within the App;
  • Your library history, including the look used and the date created;
  • Your language preference.

Information processed automatically:

  • An anonymous installation identifier generated randomly on your device. It is used solely to limit how many portraits a single device may request, is not linked to your identity, and changes if you reinstall the App;
  • Technical records of each request, limited to which model was used, how long it took, how many tokens it consumed, and whether it succeeded.

Information we do not collect:

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any account credentials;
  • We do not require account registration to use the App;
  • We do not collect precise or approximate location data;
  • We do not collect payment information;
  • We do not run third-party advertising, analytics, or crash reporting SDKs within the App;
  • We do not access your contacts, calendar, microphone, or camera roll beyond the single photograph you select.

3. How We Use Your Information

The limited information processed by the App is used solely to:

  • Generate the portrait you requested from the photograph you selected;
  • Display and store the portraits you keep within the App on your device;
  • Remember your language preference;
  • Limit the number of requests from a single device so the service remains available to everyone;
  • Respond to inquiries you send to us by email.

4. Photo Processing and Retention

When you select a photograph and request a portrait, that photograph is transmitted over an encrypted connection to our server. Our server passes it to our image processing provider, which generates the new image. The finished portrait is returned directly to your device within the same response.

Your photograph is not saved. It is held in memory only for the seconds the request takes and is discarded when the request completes. It is never written to disk, never assigned a web address, and never accessible to any other person.

The generated portrait is not saved on our servers either. It is returned to your device and discarded. The only copy is the one on your phone.

In short

We do not operate a photo library, an archive, or a backup of anything you create. There is no copy of your photograph or your portrait on our systems to be accessed, shared, or breached.

5. Data Storage and Security

All portraits you keep are stored locally on your device using the App's private storage. No portrait or photograph is transmitted to or stored on RetroMe servers.

All communication between the App and our server is encrypted in transit. The credentials used to reach the image processing provider are held only on our server and are never included in the App.

You can delete individual portraits at any time within the App, erase all App data from the Settings screen, or uninstall the App. Uninstalling the App permanently removes all locally stored data.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share any personal information with third parties.

To generate a portrait, the photograph you select is transmitted to OpenAI, which provides the image generation service. This transfer occurs solely to fulfil your request. We do not send OpenAI your name, email address, or installation identifier. OpenAI's handling of the data it receives is governed by its own privacy policy, available at openai.com/policies/privacy-policy. We do not control the retention practices of that provider.

The only other circumstances under which we may disclose information are:

  • If required to do so by law or in response to a valid legal process;
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of RetroMe or its users.

7. Purchases

If the App offers a paid upgrade or subscription, all payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive or store your card details, billing address, or purchase history. We receive only confirmation that a purchase is active.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at hussainmesum8@gmail.com and we will take appropriate steps to remove such information.

9. Third-Party Links

10. Your Rights and Choices

  • Delete any portrait from within the App;
  • Erase all App data from the Settings screen;
  • Delete all App data by uninstalling the App;

If you reside in a jurisdiction with specific data protection rights (such as the European Economic Area or California), and you believe any of those rights apply, please contact us at hussainmesum8@gmail.com.
